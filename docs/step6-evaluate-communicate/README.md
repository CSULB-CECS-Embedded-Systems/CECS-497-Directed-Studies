# Step 6 - Evaluate and Communicate

Analyze the complete body of evidence and answer the research question. Do not treat a successful demonstration alone as sufficient evaluation.

## Review gate

- Results are analyzed rather than only listed.
- Conclusions are supported by evidence.
- The research question is answered directly.
- Limitations, unsuccessful results, and threats to validity are discussed.
- Recommendations and future work follow from the findings.
- Final report, presentation, and demonstration materials are complete.
