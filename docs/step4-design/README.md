# Step 4 - Design

Complete `design-specification.md` and provide the diagrams, schematics, state machines, protocols, and experimental plan needed for implementation.

## Review gate

- Hardware and software architectures are complete and mutually consistent.
- Interfaces, signals, data formats, and operating states are defined.
- Major design choices are justified against requirements and investigation findings.
- Failure behavior and safety responses are specified.
- The experimental plan can answer the research question.
